QArt Studio — UI concepts

Three sketches of how a tool combining bit steering, error-budget spending, module-geometry rendering and real verification might feel. The previews are driven by a mock plan generator: real QR layout (finders, timing, alignment, format, version info), simulated controllable regions, residuals and a per-block error ledger. Numbers are illustrative, not from a real solver.

Concept 2

Layer stack: techniques as composable, toggleable layers

Each technique is a layer with an eye icon, like an image editor. Switching a layer off shows exactly what it contributes: turn off Bit steering and the data region reverts to noise; turn off Halftone and only module-level detail remains; turn off Budget and the residual mismatches come back. Good for teaching, and for debugging why a code does not scan.

Concept 3

Candidates & verify: search the free parameters, then prove it scans

Mask, rotation, version and offset are free choices nobody searches automatically today. Here every mask × rotation is solved and scored, laid out as a contact sheet; the best is picked by default and any can be chosen. The right side is the verification view from the toolkit lineage: a grid-aligned mismatch diff of the rendered output and a decoder matrix.
